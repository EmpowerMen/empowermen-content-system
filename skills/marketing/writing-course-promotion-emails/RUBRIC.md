# Quality Rubric: Writing Course Promotion Emails

## Pass/Fail Criteria

An email **passes** this rubric only if it meets ALL criteria below.

---

## Content Criteria

### ✅ Uses Only Course Source Material

**Pass:** Email draws exclusively from `/foundations/courses/[course-name]/modules/[module-name]/` files (workbook, transcripts, aims-and-practices, movie-assignments)

**Fail:** 
- Email contains information not in course files
- AI-generated concepts without course basis
- External quotes without attribution
- Made-up statistics or examples

**How to Check:**
- Every major claim traces back to a course file
- Reflection prompts are from course materials or clearly original
- No external sources cited without attribution

---

### ✅ One Focused Theme Per Email

**Pass:** Email centers on ONE clear topic
- Archetype Insight: ONE archetypal concept
- Shadow Work: ONE reflection question/exercise
- Storytelling: ONE movie/character
- Common Pitfalls: ONE specific shadow manifestation

**Fail:**
- Email tries to cover 2+ different topics
- Multiple reflection questions in one email
- Mixing different archetypes or concepts
- Unclear central message

**How to Check:**
- Can you summarize the email in ONE sentence?
- Is there a clear single focal point?
- Would removing any section weaken the theme?

---

### ✅ Includes Reflection Prompt

**Pass:** Email ends with ONE clear, actionable reflection question or exercise

**Fail:**
- No reflection prompt
- Vague questions with no clear action
- Multiple conflicting prompts
- CTA replaces reflection prompt

**How to Check:**
- Can the reader easily answer the prompt in 2-3 minutes?
- Is it a real question, not rhetorical?
- Would a reader know what to do with this prompt?

---

### ✅ Clear Course Call-to-Action

**Pass:** Email includes explicit CTA directing to course
- Button text: "Explore The Sovereign Man Course" or similar
- Links to course URL: `https://masculinetest.com/home/sovereign-man-a-shadow-work-online-course/`
- CTA appears once, toward end of email

**Fail:**
- No CTA or vague reference
- Multiple competing CTAs
- CTA buries course under generic language
- CTA link incorrect or missing

**How to Check:**
- Is there exactly ONE clear button or link?
- Does it direct to the course page?
- Would a reader know to click for the course?

---

## Tone & Voice Criteria

### ✅ Nurture Tone (Not Hard-Sell)

**Pass:** Email educates and inspires; promotion is implicit
- Generous with value and insight
- Invitation, not pressure
- Assumes reader is exploring, not deciding
- Focus on reader benefit, not sales

**Fail:**
- Pushy language: "Don't miss out!"
- Fear-based: "You're failing if you don't..."
- Scarcity tactics: "Only 3 spots left"
- Hype language: "Revolutionary!" "Life-changing!"
- Repeated CTAs or urgency language

**How to Check:**
- Would you want to receive this email regularly?
- Does it assume you're interested vs. doubting?
- Is the value clear without the CTA?

---

### ✅ EmpowerMen Voice

**Pass:** Email reflects EmpowerMen brand voice (see `/shared/voice.md`)
- Authentic and direct
- Grounded in psychology and archetypes
- Respectful and non-judgmental
- Focused on growth and integration

**Fail:**
- Generic/corporate tone
- Overly casual or slang-heavy
- Spiritual bypassing or toxic positivity
- Judgmental language
- Contradicts EmpowerMen values

**How to Check:**
- Does this sound like EmpowerMen (not another brand)?
- Is it grounded in real psychology?
- Is it respectful and growth-focused?

---

## Structure Criteria

### ✅ Proper Email Format

**Pass:** Email follows template structure from TEMPLATES.md
- Clear subject line (40-70 chars)
- Salutation: "Hi [First Name],"
- Opening hook (1-2 sentences)
- Body with clear sections
- Reflection prompt
- CTA
- Sign-off with closer and name

**Fail:**
- Missing sections
- Unclear structure
- Rambling or disorganized
- Missing header elements

---

### ✅ Appropriate Length

**Pass:** Email meets word count target for its type
- Archetype Insight: 200-300 words
- Shadow Work Prompt: 220-320 words
- Storytelling: 240-350 words
- Common Pitfalls: 250-360 words

**Fail:**
- Too short (< 150 words)
- Too long (> 400 words)

**How to Check:**
- Paste email into word counter
- Does it fall within range for its type?

---

### ✅ Readable Formatting

**Pass:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Bullet points for lists
- Clear section breaks
- No walls of text
- Line length appropriate for mobile (~60 chars)

**Fail:**
- Long dense paragraphs
- No visual hierarchy
- Hard to scan

---

## Accuracy Criteria

### ✅ Course Material Accuracy

**Pass:** Any quotes or paraphrases are accurate to source

**Fail:**
- Misquotes or distortions
- Out-of-context quotes
- False claims about course content

**How to Check:**
- Do any quotes match exactly to course files?
- Are concepts represented fairly?
- Would course creator recognize their content?

---

### ✅ Links and Metadata Correct

**Pass:**
- Course URL is correct
- Course name is correct
- Module name is correct
- No broken links

**Fail:**
- Wrong URLs
- Misspelled course/module names
- Broken links

---

## Checklist

Use this checklist before sending:

**Content**
- ☐ All content comes from course files
- ☐ One focused theme
- ☐ Includes reflection prompt
- ☐ Clear course CTA

**Tone**
- ☐ Nurture tone (not hard-sell)
- ☐ EmpowerMen voice
- ☐ Respectful and grounded

**Structure**
- ☐ Follows template structure
- ☐ Appropriate word count
- ☐ Readable formatting
- ☐ Clear sections

**Accuracy**
- ☐ Quotes are accurate
- ☐ Links work and are correct
- ☐ Course/module names correct
- ☐ No broken references

---

## Scoring

**Pass:** All criteria met

**Fail:** Any single criterion not met

---

## Common Fails & How to Fix

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "Email feels too promotional" | Remove sales language, focus on value |
| "Multiple topics in one email" | Cut it in half; make 2 emails |
| "No clear reflection prompt" | Add ONE specific, actionable question |
| "Quotes don't match source" | Re-read course file; paraphrase instead |
| "Email too long" | Remove examples or trim sections |
| "CTA unclear" | Use template CTA pattern from TEMPLATES.md |
