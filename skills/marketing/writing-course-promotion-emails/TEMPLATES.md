# Email Templates for Course Promotion Emails

## Template 1: Archetype Insight Email

### Structure

```
Subject: [Insight about archetype or integration]

Hi [First Name],

[Opening: Hook with powerful statement about archetype]

[Body: Expand on the concept with 2-3 key points]

[Illustration: Concrete example or scenario]

Reflection for Today:

[One reflective question for reader]

[Brief transition to course]

[CTA with button text]

Stay strong,
[Your Name]
EmpowerMen
```

### Character Limits

- Subject line: 50-70 characters
- Opening hook: 1-2 sentences
- Body: 75-150 words
- Reflection prompt: 1 question, 20-40 words
- CTA: 1 sentence + button

## Formatting Tips

1. **Use short paragraphs** (2-3 sentences max)
2. **Use bullet points** for lists and reflection prompts
3. **Bold key concepts** sparingly
4. **Use line breaks** between sections
5. **Keep line length** to ~60 characters for mobile

---

## Subject Line Tips

### What Works

- Questions: "Are You Trapped in This Shadow?"
- Bold statements: "The King Archetype's Hidden Power"
- Curiosity: "What Can King Arthur Teach You?"
- Challenges: "Build Your King Energy This Week"

### What to Avoid

- All caps: "DON'T USE CAPS"
- Emojis (unless brand-standard)
- Clickbait or false promises
- Generic sales language
