# Writing Course Promotion Emails

**Skill Category:** Marketing

**Purpose:** Create nurture and sales emails that promote EmpowerMen online courses using course materials as source content.

## Overview

This skill enables you to create focused, value-driven weekly emails that promote online courses (such as The Sovereign Man) to engaged audiences. Each email draws from course materials and follows one of several proven email types.

## When to Use This Skill

**Triggers:**
- "Create a promotional email for [course module]"
- "Write a nurture email for the King Within module"
- "Draft a weekly course promotion email"
- "Generate a shadow work prompt email"
- "I need an archetype insight email"

## Required Inputs

Before starting, gather:

1. **Course and Module** - Which course/module to draw from
   - Example: "The Sovereign Man: A Shadow Work Online Course, King Within module"

2. **Email Type** - Which type of email to create
   - Archetype Insight
   - Shadow Work Prompt
   - Storytelling / Movie Assignment
   - Common Pitfalls

3. **Specific Focus** (optional) - A particular concept, archetype, or shadow to focus on
   - Example: "The Tyrant King shadow" or "King-Warrior integration"

4. **Audience** (optional) - Who this email targets
   - Engaged course subscribers
   - Cold email list
   - Past course students

## Email Types & Workflows

### 1. Archetype Insight Email

**Purpose:** Share a powerful teaching or concept from the course archetype.

**Workflow:**
1. Select a key archetype concept from course materials (e.g., King integration with other archetypes)
2. Extract 1-2 core teaching points
3. Illustrate with a concrete example or scenario
4. End with a reflection prompt
5. Include CTA to course

**Source Materials:** Video transcripts, workbook

**Tone:** Educate and inspire, not promotional

### 2. Shadow Work Prompt Email

**Purpose:** Provide a reflective question or journaling prompt from course exercises.

**Workflow:**
1. Find a reflective prompt from the workbook or video transcripts
2. Frame it with brief context
3. Invite personal introspection
4. Offer a simple application or next step
5. Include CTA to course

**Source Materials:** Workbook exercises, video teachings

**Tone:** Inviting, curious, supportive

### 3. Storytelling / Movie Assignment Email

**Purpose:** Use archetypal characters and recommended films to illustrate lessons.

**Workflow:**
1. Reference a movie assignment from the course
2. Describe an archetypal character or scene
3. Connect character to module archetype
4. Invite reader to reflect on their own experience
5. Include CTA to course or movie assignment challenge

**Source Materials:** Movie assignments file, video transcripts

**Tone:** Narrative-driven, relatable, engaging

### 4. Common Pitfalls Email

**Purpose:** Highlight shadow manifestations and guide toward integration.

**Workflow:**
1. Identify a specific pitfall or shadow from course materials
2. Explain the manifestation clearly
3. Show the impact or consequence
4. Frame path forward toward balanced expression
5. Include CTA to course for deeper work

**Source Materials:** Video transcripts (especially Robert Moore lectures on shadows), aims-and-practices

**Tone:** Insightful, compassionate, empowering

**What you can vary:**
- Which specific teaching or exercise you focus on
- The reflection prompt (must be original or from course)
- The CTA wording (must link to course)
- Email subject line tone (value-focused, curious, or urgent)
- Personalization elements (salutation, closing)

**What must stay consistent:**
- Source material comes only from course files
- One focused theme per email
- No hard-sell language
- Proper course attribution
- EmpowerMen voice and tone (see `/shared/voice.md`)

## Tips for Success

1. **Start with the source material** - Read the course file first, then draft
2. **One idea per email** - Don't cram multiple concepts into one email
3. **Focus on value, not promotion** - The promotion is the implicit CTA, not the message
4. **Use course quotes sparingly** - Extract key ideas but write originally
5. **Test reflection prompts** - Ask yourself the question first
6. **Keep it readable** - Short paragraphs, clear sections

## Troubleshooting

**"I'm not sure which email type to use"**
→ Start with Archetype Insight or Shadow Work Prompt—they're most accessible

**"The course material feels thin for this email"**
→ You may need to combine 2-3 files (e.g., workbook + video transcript) to find enough material

**"I want to add my own interpretation"**
→ Do it! But keep source material as the anchor; your interpretation is the supporting commentary

**"Should I cite every sentence?"**
→ No. Cite concepts and direct quotes. General insights don't need attribution.
