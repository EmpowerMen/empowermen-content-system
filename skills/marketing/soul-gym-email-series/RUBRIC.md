# Soul Gym Email Series Rubric

## Quality Criteria

- Email content is under 300 words, focused, and easy to read.  
- The archetype focus is clear and consistent throughout the email.  
- The inner workout section is actionable, practical, and tied to the gym metaphor.  
- Subject line uses the "Soul Gym:" prefix and is concise and compelling.  
- Opening quote or meme is relevant and adds immediate value.  
- CTA is contextually appropriate and softly encourages engagement without hard selling.  
- Sign-off and PS statement maintain brand voice and provide additional value.  
- Tone matches the archetype energy (e.g., Warrior direct, Lover empathetic).  
- Email avoids jargon and is accessible to men new to archetypal psychology.  
- Grammar, spelling, and formatting are flawless.

## Fail Conditions

- Email exceeds 300 words or is unfocused.  
- Archetype or gym metaphor is unclear or inconsistent.  
- Subject line missing or not using the prefix.  
- CTA is overly promotional or irrelevant.  
- Tone is off-brand or confusing.  
- Poor grammar or formatting errors.
