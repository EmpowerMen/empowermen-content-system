# Soul Gym Email Series Skill

## Purpose

Create a branded, archetype-focused email series called **Soul Gym** that delivers concise, actionable inner workout tips for masculine development. The series aims to nurture leads, build brand recognition, and increase conversions for EmpowerMen’s products.

## Inputs

- Archetype focus for the email (King, Warrior, Magician, Lover)
- Specific inner workout tip or practice
- Supporting quote or meme related to masculine psychology
- Relevant call-to-action (course, free resource, community)
- Email sender name (for A/B testing options)
- Subject line prefix: "Soul Gym:"

## Outputs

- Fully formatted email copy under 300 words
- Subject line with prefix
- Preheader text (optional)
- Sign-off and PS statement

## Workflow

1. Select archetype and tip for the email.
2. Choose an opening quote or meme from the foundations or messaging library.
3. Write a concise, actionable inner workout section (under 200 words).
4. Add a contextual CTA linking to relevant EmpowerMen products or resources.
5. Include a sign-off with founder’s name.
6. Add a PS statement linking to related content (video, blog, meme).
7. Format subject line with "Soul Gym:" prefix.
8. Review for tone, clarity, and brand alignment.
9. Deliver final email copy.

## Degrees of Freedom

- Tone can vary slightly to match archetype energy (e.g., Warrior emails more direct, Lover emails more empathetic).
- Quotes and memes can be rotated for freshness.
- CTAs can be tailored to current campaigns or product launches.
