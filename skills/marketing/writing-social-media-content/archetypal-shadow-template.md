## Instructions for use

- Replace placeholders in curly braces with relevant archetypes, shadows, and descriptions.  
- Keep sentences short and direct.  
- Maintain EmpowerMen style: one idea per sentence, no em-dashes, no jargon.  
- Use this template to create posts on different psychological patterns (e.g., neediness, anger, avoidance).  
- Include a clear call to action or reflection prompt at the end.

## Example filled-in post

# The psychology of men who are needy

Neediness is a sign of disconnection from core parts of the masculine psyche.

Robert Moore’s work shows three key shadows behind neediness:

**The Weakling Prince (deflated King)**  
He cannot bless himself. He looks outside for worth and validation.

**The Masochist (deflated Warrior)**  
He cannot set boundaries. He serves others to earn love and fears rejection.

**The Impotent Lover (passive Lover shadow)**  
He is cut off from his own feelings and aliveness. He depends on others to feel alive.

This man asks, “Do you love me? Am I enough?” But the real question is whether his inner King can bless him.

Neediness is not weakness. It is deflation. It means parts of the self are offline.

The way forward is to reclaim these energies:  
- Bless yourself with King energy  
- Set boundaries with Warrior energy  
- Connect with your feelings through Lover energy  
- Observe your patterns with Magician energy

This is the path to mature masculinity — balanced, grounded, and whole.
