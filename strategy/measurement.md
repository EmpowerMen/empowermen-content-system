# Measurement

As of 13 October 2026.

## The path

Content, then the free Masculine Energy test (masculinetest.com, quiz on its subdomain), then an offer page for the workbook that matches his most out-of-balance energy ($29.99, Thinkific), then the Sovereign Man upsell ($90, Skool, four workbooks and the 60-day Master Plan). Test sign-ups land in SendPulse with their weakest energy, which drives Track 2 emails.

## Headline number

Free test completions per week, by source.

## Baselines (YouTube audit, 8 October 2026)

Shorts median 613 views and 3.6% like ratio. Long-form median 1,860 views and 3.1%. 5,350 subscribers, up 53 in 28 days. Best single video 20,551 views. The 50,000-views-in-a-week goal is about 2.5 times the best video so far, so treat it as a stretch.

## Attribution

Every YouTube video gets its own UTM slug: `?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=[video-slug]`. Ad traffic is tagged by platform at sign-up in SendPulse. Purchases are counted back to campaigns through later promo emails, not tracked through checkout.

## Review rhythm

Reviews on 14, 19 and 26 October and 9 November (see the YouTube content plan). Monthly check of the pillar weighting against fresh test data.
