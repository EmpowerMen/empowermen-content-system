# Pillars and Audience

As of 13 October 2026.

## Who we are talking to

A man aged roughly 25 to 44 who feels stuck, overwhelmed or disconnected from his own power. He may not know the word archetype and has probably never heard of Robert Moore. He searches for his problem in plain words: anxiety, can't say no, feel empty, relationships keep failing, avoid responsibility. Moore's lectures are what hold him once he arrives.

## What the test data says

More than 12,000 men have taken the free Masculine Energy test. The segment table below uses a sample of the most recent 4,000 or so test takers, treated as representative. Of those, 3,442 had an energy out of balance. Raw = over 62%, Repressed = under 37%. The balanced range is about 37 to 65.

The top three segments are 65.3% of the group. Warrior and King content leads. Magician is the third pillar. Lover content works best as a relationship-problem hook.

## Pillars

| Pillar | Who it speaks to | The man's problem, in his words | What the content gives him |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fire in the belly (Warrior) | Repressed Warrior, 24.4% | Anxious, can't say no, avoids conflict, no discipline | Courage, boundaries, healthy aggression, a reason to fight |
| Humble the throne (King) | Raw King 21.6%, Repressed King 7.3% | Needs to be right, feels like a fraud, gives his power away | Calm, centring, sacrifice, blessing others, a stoic tone |
| See clearly (Magician) | Repressed Magician, 19.3% | Avoids looking at himself, can't decide, numb to what he knows | Self-awareness, honest reflection, dreamwork, active imagination |
| Open the heart (Lover) | Raw and Repressed Lover, 18% | Relationships fail, emotional numbness, chasing, keeping options open | Connection, desire with direction, feeling without drowning |

**Two doors** bring in men who don't know the framework yet.

- **Man-child:** puer aeternus and the father wound as the plain-language way into Warrior and King material. Sources include Corneau's *Absent Fathers, Lost Sons*, Bradshaw's *Home-Coming* and Moore on the puer.
- **Figures in the news:** the four energies as a lens on public behaviour, ending on the mirror (what is true of me). Public record only, non-partisan, never a diagnosis, skip anyone facing live criminal allegations.

**One practice layer** turns every piece into something he can do. Each module's `aims-and-practices.md`, workbook and movie assignments supply the practice.

## Weighting for public (Discoverable) content

About 30% Warrior, 25% King, 20% Magician, 15% Lover, 10% open for doors and experiments. This follows the test data and should move when the data does. Review monthly.

## Anatomy of every piece

Problem hook, Moore-based insight, the mirror ("where is this in you?"), one practice, one call to action. A piece that skips the problem hook is a Community piece, not a Discoverable one.

## Tone by pillar

Warrior content should feel like a push. King content should feel like a steadying hand. Magician content should feel like a good question. Lover content should feel like honesty with warmth.
