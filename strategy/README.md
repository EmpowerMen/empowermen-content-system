# EmpowerMen Content Strategy

Operating plan for EmpowerMen content. Status: v1, 13 October 2026. Owner: Robbie.

This folder sits above the skills. The skills say how to write a piece. This folder says what to make, for whom, on which channel, and how often. Read it before planning or drafting any content.

## The strategy in one paragraph

EmpowerMen content puts fire in the belly of men who are stuck in boy psychology, and turns that spark into one action: taking the free Masculine Energy test. The mission phrase comes from Sam Keen's *Fire in the Belly*. The brand promise, used as a sign-off, is "In strength, endurance, and wisdom."

**The one number to watch:** free test completions per week, traced to the piece or ad that sent each man.

## Rules that hold across everything

- One call to action per piece. The free test is the default.
- Cold audiences get problems in their own words. Warm audiences get the framework.
- Every piece is a lens, not a diagnosis. Keep the not-therapy line on anything touching anxiety, depression, fathers or public figures.
- Moore quotes come only from the real transcripts in `foundations/robert-moore-lectures/transcripts/`, and are checked against the audio before use.
- Use "Weakling", not "Weakling Prince". Never use "Naif"; the Magician's passive shadow is "Denying Innocent One".
- Voice and style rules are in `shared/voice.md` and `shared/style.md`.
