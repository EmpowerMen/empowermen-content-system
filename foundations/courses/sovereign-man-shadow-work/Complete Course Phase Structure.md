# Course Phase Structure

---

### **Phase 1: KING WITHIN** (Days 1-14)

**Command —** Primary goal of helping you lead your life with calm authority.  
Learn to establish respect and centered leadership in your family and daily life. Develop the ability to make clear decisions and create a vision that guides your actions with confidence and integrity.

---

### **Phase 2: WARRIOR WITHIN** (Days 15-28)

**Defend —** Focuses on setting and maintaining boundaries that actually hold.  
Build discipline, courage, and the capacity to stand firm in difficult situations. Learn to protect your time, energy, and values with strength and clarity, avoiding both passivity and destructive aggression.

---

### **Phase 3: MAGICIAN WITHIN** (Days 29-42)

**Strategize —** Develop clear thinking and the ability to read situations accurately.  
Gain strategic insight to avoid manipulation and make wise decisions. Cultivate awareness of hidden dynamics in relationships and work, enabling you to navigate life’s complexities with wisdom and foresight.

---

### **Phase 4: LOVER WITHIN** (Days 43-56)

**Connect —** Reconnect with your emotions, vitality, and relationships.  
Learn to feel alive, present, and deeply connected with what matters most — your partner, children, and yourself. Cultivate passion, joy, and emotional sensitivity to enrich your life and relationships.

---

### **Phase 5: SOVEREIGN MAN MASTER PLAN** (Days 57-58)

**Take Control —** Integrate all four archetypes into a cohesive, actionable plan.  
Consolidate your learning and growth into a personalized roadmap for sustained transformation. Begin living fully, with purpose, balance, and the sovereignty to lead your life on your terms.

---

This phased structure provides clear milestones and actionable focus areas, helping men progress steadily from establishing authority to living a balanced, empowered life.
