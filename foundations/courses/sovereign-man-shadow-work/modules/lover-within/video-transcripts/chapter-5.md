### Create Your Lover Energy Action Plan

Welcome to Chapter 5, the final part of the Lover Within Module.

In previous chapters, we’ve explored your shadow — the dark, repressed side of the Lover archetype within your psyche. The way out of feelings of addiction, obsession, or emotional detachment lies in confronting this shadow. By bringing light and awareness to your unconscious behaviors, patterns, and limiting beliefs, you can begin to retrieve and awaken the Lover energy that has been lost in your shadow.

Now, it’s time to create an action plan. But before we do, let’s explore what it means to embody the Lover in his fullness.

---

### Embodying the Lover in His Fullness

The Lover in his fullness embodies several key qualities. Consider these as potential aims for your own journey:

- **Cultivate the virtue of moderation:** Practice balance in all aspects of life. This could mean enjoying life’s pleasures without overindulgence, like savoring a glass of wine rather than drinking excessively.
- **Become fully present:** Engage in practices to enhance your ability to live in the moment. Try focusing on your senses during everyday activities, like really tasting your food or feeling the warmth of the sun on your skin. In other words, slow down and smell the roses!
- **Find the centre of your bliss:** Discover what truly brings you joy and fulfillment. This might involve exploring different hobbies or revisiting childhood passions you’ve neglected.
- **Be spontaneous:** Allow yourself to break from routine and embrace unexpected experiences. This could be as simple as taking a different route home or trying a new cuisine.
- **Work out what your lover needs:** Develop emotional intelligence and empathy in your relationships. Practice active listening and ask your partner about their needs and desires.

Based on these objectives for a balanced Lover, and considering all the exercises you’ve completed so far, which of these aims resonate most with you? What is your gut telling you? Take a moment to reflect and make note of these in your workbook.

Remember, this is your unique journey to awakening your inner Lover. Trust your instincts and be honest with yourself as you create your action plan.

### Creating a Plan

Let’s create a basic action plan for cultivating your Lover energy. Follow this step-by-step process and turn to Workbook Exercise 4 in your Lover Within workbook.

**Step One: Ignite Your Insights**  
Look back at your previous notes in the workbook. Reflect on the insights you’ve gathered throughout the course. Recognize your progress and personal work so far. Prepare mentally for the next steps in your development.

**Step Two: Target Your Lover Energy**  
Identify areas to focus on improving. What actions did you make notes of when facing your Lover shadow? What gaps did you identify in your Lover energy wheel? What observations did you make when assessing your Lover energy? Which of the aims of the Lover in his fullness resonated deeply with you?  
Make a note of your aims. For example, it could be “Work out what your lover needs,” “Find my bliss,” or “Slow down and smell the roses.” Write just a short phrase for your aim, so that it’s easy to keep in mind. Remember, this must be personalized because nobody else is living your life! The aim description is flexible so you can write something that fits your personal circumstances around your Lover energy insights.

**Step Three: Spark Your Passion**  
Choose an action, activity, or practice you could do to help support and achieve your aim. We have an ever-growing list of ideas and examples to help you in this chapter, in an article following this video. We also encourage members of the EmpowerMen community to contribute ideas and suggestions that have worked for them, so this list is a co-creation and a meeting of minds of men supporting each other in their personal development. Visit the relevant section in our Discord community to get involved. Go through the list and make a note in your workbook of the practices you’d like to do, next to your aim.

**Step Four: Embrace Vulnerability**  
To develop the aspects of your Lover archetype that need growth, you must, as Dr. Robert Moore suggests, “Face the Dragon.” In this context, it means confronting your emotional barriers and embracing the vulnerability that comes with deep connection. Don’t simply choose practices or activities that feel safe or superficial. For the greatest development and growth, push yourself to open your heart in ways that might initially feel uncomfortable or even frightening. Explore emotional territories and forms of intimacy that you might instinctively shy away from — these are the areas where you’re likely to discover the most profound connections and self-understanding.

**Step Five: Strategize Your Lover Week**  
Plan ahead. Give yourself a week from now to explore your Lover energy and the actions you have identified. Plot in days and times you intend to commit to do your actions before the next module is available. Explore the homework assignment and resources in your Lover Within workbook too. Pick a movie to watch one day and complete the workbook exercise with it. Download the Active Imagination resource and plan a time to try and connect and dialogue with the Lover within. Use the Dream Work resource as a guide to interpreting your dreams through the week, watching for messages from the Lover within. And join the conversation in our EmpowerMen Community for support and ideas for creating and evolving your action plan.

**Step Six: Adapt Your Lover Lessons**  
Be flexible with your plan. You’re just trying out new things at this point to see what works for you. Remain open-minded about your chosen practices and activities, and be willing to adjust your plan as you discover what works best for you. Embrace the experimental nature of this phase in your development. Allow yourself the freedom to adjust and adapt as you go along, and trust your instincts and experiences as you refine your approach.
