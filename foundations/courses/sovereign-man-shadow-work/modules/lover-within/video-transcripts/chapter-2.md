### Exploring Your Lover Archetype

There are no right or wrong answers to the following questions. They are simply designed to give you some insight into the strength of the Lover archetype within you.

If you long for connection, or you are drawn to spiritual practice, meditation, have a “dreamy” nature, feel a strong connection with the world around you (or a connection with something greater than yourself), find sensual pleasure through enjoyment of the arts, music, and literature, or are strongly drawn to sexual expression, or if you consider yourself generally sensitive and/or artistic, your predominant archetypal energy may be that of the Lover.

Consider these questions and see if you can identify with the Lover in either its balanced, or its shadow side — in other words, with the Lover in an inflated or deflated state.

One powerful expression of the Lover’s desire for connection is through sexual expression, especially sexual “union” with another person. These questions might give you some insight into the strength of the Lover within you.

- Would you describe yourself as a skilled lover?
- Do you spend a lot of time and energy on pursuing and achieving sexual pleasure?
- Do you regard sexual ability, technique and skill as one of your greatest strengths?
- Are you obsessed with sexual prowess and experiences?
- Have you ever experienced a sense of union with your partner, or with the divine (or, perhaps, something you would describe as “greater than self”) during your lovemaking?

The Lover’s most fundamental (though often unconscious) wish is often to connect or even merge with another person, or with something “bigger than self”. Your thoughts and answers may give you some insight into the strength of this archetypal energy within you.

### Compassion

Compassion is often a sign that a person has a strong Lover.

- How compassionate are you?
- Do you find it easy to bring your compassion to anyone, regardless of what they have done?
- How judgmental are you towards those who appear to have damaged or wounded in any way somebody or something you value (including yourself)?

- Can you love yourself or others unconditionally?

### The Inflated Lover (The Addict)

Lover energy put into shadow can inflate or deflate.

Signs of an inflated Lover include:

- Feeling desperate for connection and doing things later regretted or felt ashamed of.
- Acting out of neediness or desperation for connection.
- Seeking attention in groups through ill-judged “gifts” or performances.
- Childhood deficits of love and attention, emotional neglect, or inconsistent care.
- Feeling exhausted by life’s struggles.
- Turning to addictive behaviors like gambling, drinking, overeating, drugs, or sexual excess.

---

### The Deflated Lover (The Impotent)

Signs of a deflated Lover include:

- Emotional detachment or intellectualizing emotions.
- Being seen as emotionally distant or remote.
- Difficulty understanding or valuing love and connection.
- Preferring solitude to avoid complications.
- Difficulty maintaining long-term relationships or prematurely ending them.
- Past hurts sabotaging relationships.

---

### Shadow Work

To face your Lover shadow, explore tendencies toward unhealthy attachments, codependency, or objectification of others. Foster healthy boundaries and genuine, respectful connections.
