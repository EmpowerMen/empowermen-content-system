### Identify Areas of Your Lover Energy for Development

Welcome to Chapter 3 of The Lover Within module. This video accompanies Workbook Exercise 2, where we’ll identify areas of your Lover energy for development.

Let’s begin by exploring Dr. Robert Moore’s concept of developmental lines, focusing on the Lover quadrant. Moore describes this as the instinctual line of development of sexuality, affiliation, intimacy, embodiment, and joy. Without a connection to this energy, there’s no “dance” in your life, regardless of how smart or caring you are.

The Lover archetype represents a person in touch with their feelings, expressing joy, pain, anger, and fear spontaneously. It’s about not bottling up or covering over what you feel.

In your workbook exercise, we’ve adapted Dr. Moore’s work into a wheel-of-life style assessment. You’ll score yourself in various areas based on your personal satisfaction level. We’ve also added a crucial component: devotion.

By devotion, we mean a commitment and dedication to someone or something beyond ourselves. This could be your children if you’re a parent, or Jesus if you’re a Christian, for example. Basically, something that you love.

Devotion is a selfless act, a focus on “another”, rather than ourselves. In his book, *To Love and Be Loved*, Sam Keen describes this first step into Lover energy as devoting your attention:  
*“The decision to pay attention to someone is the first act of self-limitation, the first sacrifice, the first gift we make in the name of love.”*

This exercise will help you identify your strengths and areas for improvement within your Lover energy. By understanding where you excel and where you can grow, you’ll be better equipped to develop a more balanced and mature masculine presence.

Remember, this self-assessment is a powerful tool for personal transformation. As you work through the exercise, be honest with yourself. Recognizing areas for growth is not a weakness — it’s the first step towards becoming an effective Lover in your own life.

### Completing the Lover Energy Wheel Exercise

Let’s begin by completing the Lover Energy Wheel Exercise in your workbook.

This exercise will help you assess your current Lover energy across six crucial categories.

For each of the six categories, draw a line across the segment representing your satisfaction score. Use a scale from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 10 (fully satisfied). Trust your gut feeling rather than your assumptions.

Be honest with yourself, as this will provide the most valuable insights for your journey towards embodying mature Lover energy.

A helpful tip: Pretend the score 7 isn’t available. This will push you to decide if you’re truly satisfied or dissatisfied in each area.

### The Six Categories Explained

- **Devotion:** A deep commitment and dedication to someone or something beyond ourselves.
- **Embodiment:** The ability to fully inhabit and embrace your physicality, sensuality, and emotions.
- **Erotic Sensuality:** More than just sexual desire; embodying pleasure, delight, beauty, and embracing life’s richness.
- **Intimacy:** Deep connections with others, opening up to vulnerability, creating trust and emotional closeness.
- **Community:** A sense of belonging and connection with others, recognizing interdependence and collective wellbeing.
- **Joy:** The expression of pure delight and happiness, finding pleasure in life’s simple moments.

### Identifying Strengths and Gaps

Reflect on your daily life and identify activities, habits, or behaviors that align with the Lover archetype. Consider how these practices contribute to your sense of passion, intimacy, and deep emotional connections.

List the things you already do in your life that fit within each category. Some activities may apply to multiple areas. Some sections may be fuller than others — that’s fine.

If you have nothing to add to a category, simply move on.

Examples:

- **Devotion:** Being a husband, committed to your partner.
- **Embodiment:** Practicing yoga or other body awareness activities.
- **Erotic Sensuality:** Visiting galleries, enjoying art and music.
- **Intimacy:** Having deep conversations with close friends.
- **Community:** Volunteering in local projects.
- **Joy:** Attending dance classes or engaging in playful activities.

Review your entries and notice any imbalances. These likely reflect your satisfaction levels in the wheel scores. Areas with fewer entries may indicate opportunities for growth.
