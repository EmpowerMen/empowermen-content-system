## MOVIE ASSIGNMENTS

Choose from one of the films listed below or find one of your own featuring a male character who embodies the Lover archetype. Watch attentively, focusing on how the Lover archetype is portrayed. After viewing, complete the reflective exercises provided in the course to deepen your understanding and engage in shadow work.

### Recommended Lover Archetype Films (Sorted by IMDb Rating):

1. **American Beauty (1999)** — 8.3  
Kevin Spacey’s character undergoes a journey of self-liberation, reflecting on desires and relationships in a world of facades.

2. **La La Land (2016)** — 8.0  
A musical romance exploring the passionate, sometimes painful balance between love and personal ambition.

3. **Her (2013)** — 7.9  
Joaquin Phoenix’s character develops an unlikely relationship with an AI operating system, exploring love and connection.

4. **Zorba the Greek (1964)** — 7.8  
Anthony Quinn as Zorba embraces life with passion and an insatiable thirst for experiences.

5. **Last Tango in Paris (1972)** — 7.7  
Marlon Brando’s character embodies the Lover archetype through a passionate and complex affair.

6. **A Star is Born (2018)** — 7.6  
A heartfelt story of love, sacrifice, and emotional depth between a seasoned musician and an emerging star.

7. **The Big Sick (2017)** — 7.5  
Based on a true story, this romantic comedy-drama explores cultural differences, vulnerability, and the power of love.

8. **All That Jazz (1979)** — 7.5  
Roy Scheider portrays a womanizing, drug-abusing Broadway director with complex personal struggles.

9. **Sons and Lovers (1960)** — 7.1  
Paul Morel navigates intense family and romantic relationships, influenced by an overbearing mother.

10. **Sex, Lies, and Videotape (1989)** — 7.0  
Andie MacDowell’s character becomes entangled in intricate and secretive relationships.

11. **Play Misty for Me (1971)** — 7.0  
Jessica Walter’s character obsessively pursues a DJ, embodying intense romantic infatuation.

12. **Internal Affairs (1990)** — 6.7  
Richard Gere’s character manipulates complex relationships within a web of police corruption.

13. **Don Jon (2013)** — 6.5  
Joseph Gordon-Levitt’s character reflects a skewed perception of romantic relationships.

14. **Inside Out (1986)** — 6.5  
An agoraphobic man ventures outside his sheltered life after personal and financial challenges.

15. **Another 9 1/2 Weeks (1997)** — 6.4  
Mickey Rourke’s character navigates an intense and unconventional relationship.

### Reflective Exercise Prompts:

1. Describe one negative or “shadow” trait exhibited by the Lover character. How does this trait impact the story or other characters?  
2. Reflect on a moment where you related to or were challenged by the Lover’s actions. What does this reveal about your own relationship with the archetype?  
3. Identify a person who embodies similar Lover qualities. How do you respond to their energy or advice?  
4. Identify one positive quality you’d like to develop or one shadow aspect to address. How might you begin this integration?
