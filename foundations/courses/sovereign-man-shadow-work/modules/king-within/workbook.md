### The King Archetype

**Attributes:**  
Keeping order, seeing the big picture, wise, balancing the other three archetypal energies, harmony, stability, centeredness, calmness, blessing, inclusive caring, affirmation of others, humble.

**Overview:**  
The King archetype is all about leadership, creating order, and justice. It is closely linked to being a strong and just leader. The symbolic King represents our instinctual need for nurturing and inner balance. This aspect of personal development is crucial for nurturing and a sense of blessing, influencing our emotional stability and self-awareness. Without it, you may struggle to feel calm, centered, or have a clear sense of identity and purpose.

**Finding balance and fulfillment:**  
By actively empowering those around you instead of seeking power and control, you can foster a sense of collective strength and growth. Acknowledging and respecting your feelings allows you to cultivate emotional intelligence and authenticity, leading to deeper connections and a more integrated sense of self. Striving for a healthy relationship dynamic based on empowerment and cooperation rather than dominance or control creates an environment of prosperity and respect. Embracing the royal couple archetype encourages nurturing both the king and queen aspects within yourself and your relationships, fostering balance and harmony. Cultivating a mythic sensibility, understanding mythic archetypes such as the king, guides your personal development and relationships, enriching your understanding of yourself and others. By embracing these principles, a man can navigate and integrate his King energy, promoting personal development, healthy relationships, and a profound sense of fulfillment.

### Exploring Your King Energy

The first video in the King Within module explains the archetype, followed by a video including your first workbook exercise.

**Assess your current King energy:**  
Explore the shadow side of your King energy and identify if you are more on the inflated or deflated side. This transformative journey of self-discovery is guided by Rod Boothroyd, author and shadow work facilitator.

**Workbook exercise 1:**  
Reflect on the shadow aspects of your inner King. Be honest and write freely.

- King in His Fullness: Do you have areas to improve? (Yes / No / A mix)

- Weakling Shadow: Do you have areas to improve? (Yes / No / A mix)

- “I recognize the Tyrant in me when...”

- “To develop my inner King, I need to...”  
- “The leader I aspire to be is someone who...”

### Workbook Exercise 2: Identify Areas for Development

- Ordering and Provision

List current activities, habits, or behaviors that align with King energy in these areas. Empty areas indicate development opportunities.

### Shadow King Dynamics

The Shadow King fluctuates between inflated (Tyrant) and deflated (Weakling) states:

- **Tyrant:** Tyranny, arrogance, neglect, defensiveness, grandiosity, fear of weakness, controlling behavior.  
- **Weakling:** Abdication of responsibility, indecision, weakness, fear of failure, avoidance of conflict, dependency.
