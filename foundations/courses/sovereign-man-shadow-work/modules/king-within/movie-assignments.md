## MOVIE ASSIGNMENTS

Choose from one of the films listed below or find one of your own featuring a character who embodies the King archetype. Watch the film attentively, focusing on how the King archetype is portrayed. After viewing, complete the reflective exercises provided in the course to deepen your understanding and engage in shadow work.

### Recommended King Archetype Films (Sorted by IMDb Rating):

- **The Godfather (1972)** — 9.2  
  Vito and Michael Corleone as heads of a family empire, balancing power, protection, and legacy with shadow elements.

- **The Lion King (1994)** — 8.5  
  An animated classic portraying Simba’s journey from prince to wise king, emphasizing responsibility, leadership, and legacy.

- **Gladiator (2000)** — 8.5  
  Maximus exhibits King qualities alongside Warrior traits in his leadership and quest for justice.

- **Lawrence of Arabia (1962)** — 8.3  
  The story of T.E. Lawrence’s leadership and vision during World War I, reflecting complex King archetype qualities.

- **Dead Poets Society (1989)** — 8.1  
  John Keating (Robin Williams) embodies the King archetype by empowering and inspiring his students through unconventional teaching.

- **Patton (1970)** — 7.9  
  Depicts General George S. Patton’s military leadership during World War II, showcasing authoritative and decisive King qualities.

- **Captain Fantastic (2016)** — 7.9  
  A father raising his six children in the wilderness, embodying nurturing patriarch qualities.

- **A Man for All Seasons (1966)** — 7.7  
  Sir Thomas More’s principled leadership and moral integrity exemplify King virtues of justice and ethics.

- **The Last Emperor (1987)** — 7.7  
  A biographical film exploring the burdens and challenges of kingship through Pu Yi’s life.

- **Julius Caesar (1953)** — 7.7  
  Shakespeare’s play adaptation focusing on Julius Caesar’s powerful and ambitious rule.

- **The Last Temptation of Christ (1988)** — 7.6  
  Explores the life and struggles of Jesus Christ as a spiritual leader and compassionate guide.

- **Excalibur (1981)** — 7.4

- **Camelot (1967)** — 7.5  
  A musical depicting King Arthur’s quest to create a just and chivalrous kingdom.

- **The Queen (2006)** — 7.3

- **The King (2019)** — 7.2  
  Timothée Chalamet portrays King Henry V’s transformation from reluctant prince to wise and courageous ruler.

- **King Arthur: Legend of the Sword (2017)** — 6.7

- **The Last King (2016)** — 6.6

- **King David (1985)** — 6.1  
  Portrays the biblical story of King David’s rise and struggles.

### Reflective Exercise Prompts:

1. **Shadow Trait Analysis:**  
   Identify one negative or “shadow” trait exhibited by the King character. How does this trait affect the story or other characters?

2. **Personal Connection:**  
   Reflect on a moment in the film where you related to or were challenged by the King’s actions. What does this reveal about your own relationship with the King archetype?

3. **Role Model Identification:**  
   Think of someone in your life or a public figure who embodies similar King qualities. How do you respond to their leadership style?

4. **Integration Goals:**  
   Based on your observations, identify one positive quality you want to develop or a shadow aspect to address within yourself. How might you begin this integration?
