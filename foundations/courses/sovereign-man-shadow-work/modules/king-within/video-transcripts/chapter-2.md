### Exploring Your King Archetype

The king archetype is the part of us that, when fully developed, is balanced, mature and capable of running our inner and outer world with strength and compassion.

With a strong king, we will feel confident, assured, competent and in control.

Unfortunately, for many people, their upbringing does not lead to the development of a strong king, and instead, the energy of the king is repressed or undeveloped.

Here are some questions which may help you understand the relative strength or weakness of this part of your archetypal structure. Make (and keep) notes in your workbook in whatever way will be most useful to you.

If you can answer yes to these questions, you probably have a strong, balanced king archetype.

- Do you feel calm, confident, and in control most of the time?
- When you are challenged about your actions, words or deeds, do you remain “calm and collected” and able to mount a rational defence of what you have done, and why you’ve done it?
- Are you emotionally stable and self-assured?
- In general, are your emotions well-regulated, appropriate in intensity to the situation, and expressed cleanly? In other words, are spontaneous and uncontrolled emotional outbursts, especially of anger, something you rarely, if ever, experience?
- Do you take full responsibility and hold yourself accountable when necessary for the consequences of your actions?
- Do you own your actions and their outcomes?
- Do you have a clear vision for your future, with an action plan for getting to that point, or at least moving towards it?
- Is your future mapped out with clear goals?
- If you are responsible for employees, children, or people in another setting, do you engender respect and appreciation for your leadership?
- Does your leadership inspire respect and gratitude?
- Would you say that you’re a good, solid, competent leader in your own life?
- Are you good at making decisions and taking control in a crisis?
- Are you a capable leader in your own life and crises?
- Do you have a balanced view of humanity, which encapsulates a heart-centered view of people regardless of their actions in the world?
- And do you have a spiritual practice or belief system?
- Do you view humanity with compassion and spiritual depth?

King energy develops early in life, but it can be suppressed if children are not encouraged to blossom into the fullness of themselves, or they are discouraged from expressing their king qualities. When suppressed, this energy may either inflate or deflate.

### The Inflated King (The Tyrant)

Inflated kings do not express a balanced type of king energy. Rather, they are channelling an immature, distorted form of their king energy, which has become corrupted in some way in the time since it was repressed into shadow. (That repression happens during childhood because of the attitudes of the child’s carers, siblings, peers and wider society.)

The chief sign of an inflated king is grandiosity in one form or another. This may be seen as arrogance, self-assurance that is not justified by reality, a refusal to compromise, an inability to listen to anyone else, or downright intolerance of anything or anyone who differs in any way from the supposed value system of the inflated king.

This behaviour is an expression of the belief: “I am right and you are wrong”, no matter how that idea is expressed.

The old saying “It’s my way or the highway” sums up much of the inflated king’s approach to life. What hides beneath this arrogance is a deficient sense of self-esteem or self-worth; even, in the most extreme cases, a complete absence of any genuine self-worth whatsoever.

Grandiosity may, in its ultimate form, be expressed in verbal or physical attacks on anyone who disagrees with the inflated king’s point of view. Sometimes an inflated king manifests as a demagogue or a tyrant.

Democracy means nothing to them, and they seek to overthrow established norms for their own ends, regardless of who may get hurt in the process and uncaring of what may be destroyed.

- Do you consistently believe that your opinions and ideas are always right?
- Are you always convinced of your own rightness?
- Do you often launch verbal or physical attacks on those who have a different point of view to yours?
- Do you lash out at those with different opinions?
- Do you often put others down or make fun of people?
- Do you frequently belittle or mock others?
- Are you resistant to seeking help or support from others, believing that you can always stand on your own two feet?
- Do you find it challenging to express your emotions or discuss how you’re feeling with others, instead preferring to bottle things up?
- Do you resist help and hide your emotions?
- Do you often take it personally and feel deeply affected by any negative feedback?
- Does criticism hit you hard?
- Do you believe in aiming for perfection, setting extremely high standards for yourself and becoming very critical of any perceived flaws or mistakes?
- Are you a perfectionist to a fault?

You may have recognised many of those traits in yourself, or maybe a mix.

### The Deflated King (The Weakling)

In contrast to the Inflated form of this archetype, hiding one’s self from view is the key sign of a deflated king. This may be a literal hiding away or a metaphorical one: a lack of desire, an inability, even, to “stand up and be counted”, let alone be seen or heard.

Anything that may bring down the disapproval, scorn, punishment or abuse of childhood is avoided - generally, this means not being anywhere, or doing anything, that might attract attention. Often deflated king energy leads a person to a place where they do not know what they stand for; they cannot say what they think or feel or believe about an issue, and they are reluctant to express an opinion that might cause offence.

At the core of this deflation, there is a lack of self-knowing. It follows that deflated kings will not make good leaders and will not command respect.

They may not be able to plan for the future or run their own lives properly, let alone anyone else’s. They will generally not have any clarity about what values or principles they stand for, and they’re unlikely to have a sense of mission and purpose in life.

- Do you often avoid situations where attention will be on you?
- Do you shy away from the spotlight?
- Are you reluctant to express your opinions on important matters fearing disapproval from others?
- Do you silence your views to avoid disapproval?
- Have you never thought about what your personal values are or what your purpose in life is?
- Do you have a sense of your values and life purpose?
- Do you struggle to do any planning for the future?
- Is future planning a challenge for you?
- Do you find it challenging to take charge and lead, or to command respect and make decisions?
- Do you struggle to lead and command respect?

If you answered yes to these questions, it’s a sign your King energy is under-functioning.
