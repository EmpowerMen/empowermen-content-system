### Embodying the King in His Fullness

The King in his fullness embodies several key qualities. Consider these as potential aims for your own action plan:

- **Leave a legacy:** The balanced King aims to leave a legacy, to create a story revered through time. Yours could be raising a family, with fond stories passed down to your grandchildren.
- **Develop practical wisdom:** Seek knowledge and apply it wisely in daily life, making decisions benefiting yourself and others. Wisdom comes from experience, reflection, and learning from successes and failures.
- **Become a mentor:** Share your knowledge and experience, guiding others on their journeys. A true King understands his legacy lives on through those he inspires and empowers.
- **Find a mentor:** Seek guidance from those who have walked the path before you. Even Kings need counsel; humility in seeking wisdom is a sign of true strength.
- **Develop the virtue of order:** Create structure in your life and environment, fostering stability and growth. This order extends beyond physical spaces to thoughts, relationships, and goals.
- **Break away from your mother:** Establish independence and define your identity separate from childhood caregiver expectations. This process often requires consciously rejecting limiting roles and embracing full masculine potential.
- **Develop traits of true leadership:** Inspire and guide others through actions and decisions. A King leads through example, compassion, and bringing out the best in those around him.
- **Establish your core values:** Define what truly matters and live by these principles. These values will be your North Star, guiding difficult decisions and shaping your personal kingdom.
- **Develop a life plan:** Set clear goals and create a roadmap for achieving them. A King without vision is merely a figurehead; your life plan is the blueprint for your legacy.
- **Protect the sanctity of your ideas:** Value your thoughts and creativity, nurturing them into reality. Guard your unique perspective against naysayers and cultivate it with care.
- **Become decisive:** Make clear, confident decisions and stand by them. A King who wavers loses trust; act with conviction while remaining open to new information.
- **Avoid corruption:** Maintain integrity even in adversity. True power comes from character strength and respect, not exploitation.
- **Live with integrity:** Align actions with words and values. This congruence earns trust and admiration.

### Step-by-Step Process

**Step One: Ignite Your Insights**  
Review your previous workbook notes, reflect on your progress, and prepare mentally for the next steps.

**Step Two: Target Your King Energy**  
Identify areas for improvement from your shadow work and energy wheel. Choose a personalized aim (e.g., “Live with integrity,” “Develop ordering and provision,” “Handle criticism better”). Write a short, flexible phrase to keep in mind.

**Step Three: Empower Your Kingdom**  
Select actions or practices to support your aim. Use the provided list and community suggestions. Note chosen practices in your workbook.

**Step Four: Embrace the Responsibility**  
Face your fears and challenges. Take on leadership and difficult decisions to grow your King energy. Push beyond comfort zones to find growth.

**Step Five: Strategize Your King Week**  
Plan a week to explore your King energy. Schedule times for actions, use resources like Active Imagination and Dream Work, watch a King archetype movie, and engage with the EmpowerMen Community.

**Step Six: Adapt Your Sovereign Strategy**  
Be flexible and open-minded. Adjust your plan as you discover what works best. Embrace experimentation and trust your instincts.
