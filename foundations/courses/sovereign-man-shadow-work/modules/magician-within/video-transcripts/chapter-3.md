As Robert Moore puts it in his book, *The Magician Within*, the man accessing his Magician energy “…makes time to be alone with himself, with his thoughts and feelings. By doing so he maintains communication with his own unconscious. Others gravitate to him, because of his inner order and calm.”

This exercise will help you identify your strengths and areas for improvement within your Magician energy. By understanding where you excel and where you can grow, you’ll be better equipped to develop a more balanced and mature masculine presence.

### Completing the Magician Energy Wheel Exercise

This exercise will help you assess your current Magician energy across six crucial categories.

For each of the six categories, draw a line across the segment representing your satisfaction score. Use a scale from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 10 (fully satisfied). Trust your gut feeling rather than your assumptions.

Remember, this exercise is about self-reflection and growth. Be honest with yourself, as this will provide the most valuable insights for your journey towards embodying mature Magician energy.

Take your time to score each category thoughtfully. Once you’ve completed the wheel, we’ll move on to the second part of Workbook Exercise 2, where we’ll analyze your current situation and identify your strengths and areas for improvement.

Here’s a helpful tip: Pretend the score 7 isn’t available. This will push you to decide if you’re truly satisfied or dissatisfied in each area.

### The Six Categories Explained

- **Mindfulness:** Being aware of your thoughts, emotions, and surroundings and your ability to create a calm space for reflection and careful decision-making. How satisfied are you with your ability to do this for your own wellbeing and emotional balance?
- **Knowledge:** Your accumulation of information and understanding.
- **Transformation:** Personal growth, learning from disappointments, and elevating yourself to a higher level of understanding and mastery.
- **Contemplation:** Your ability to access inner wisdom and cultivate clear, careful thinking.
- **Wisdom:** Your application of knowledge and experience to make sound judgments.
- **Healing:** Your ability to release past wounds, find inner balance, and cultivate emotional wellbeing, enabling you to share your wisdom with others.
