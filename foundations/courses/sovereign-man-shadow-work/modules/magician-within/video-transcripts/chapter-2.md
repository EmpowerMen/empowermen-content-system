### Exploring Your Magician Energy

The following questions may help you to understand what form of the Magician energy is the dominant one in your psyche.

The Magician is a part of the psyche associated with thinking and analyzing, understanding and working things out, planning, and problem-solving. It is primarily a mental energy, rather than an action-based energy like the Warrior or an emotional energy like the Lover.

As you hear the following questions, it may be helpful to jot a few notes down for future reference, so you have a greater understanding of how you think and relate to the world around you.

If you answer yes to all these questions, you are likely to have predominant Magician energy.

- Do you enjoy solving puzzles?
- Do you feel safer if you know the answer to every question (or at least the important ones)?
- Have you always sought to understand what is happening in your world and the wider world, and why it is happening?
- Do you enjoy intellectual challenges?
- Does understanding lead to a sense of satisfaction?
- When you were young, did you have to stay “one step ahead” of the people around you for any reason?
- Do you feel unable to understand, or have a negative opinion about people who seem very emotional?
- Do you feel the need to excel?
- Do you believe that arguments should be won on the basis of sound rational debate and logic, not force of will or a sense of dominance?
- Do you value debate over dominance?
- Do you lead, take part in, or otherwise enjoy ceremonies designed to access different states of consciousness?
- Do you explore your consciousness?
- Do you enjoy a good intellectual challenge?
- Were you shamed for any reason, even perhaps for your very existence, when you were a child?
- Would you say you are intellectually clever, or that you have a lot of “common sense”?
- Can you see through other people’s lies and deceit?
- Are you a perceptive person?
- One major energy of the Magician is anxiety or fear, which can act as a sign that one has a problem and needs to find a solution. Are you an anxious person?
- Have you spent a lot of your life feeling anxious, or waking up in the morning with a high level of anxiety?
- Do you experience anxiety regularly?

Overall, what is your sense of your Magician archetype? Would you describe it as strong, mature and balanced, under your control and effective in taking action when needed? If not, continue watching on and see if you can recognize some of the characteristics of the Inflated or Deflated Magician as described.

### The Inflated Magician (The Detached Manipulator)

Like all archetypal energy, Magician energy which is put into shadow can inflate or deflate.

If you can answer yes to the majority of the questions below, you may have strong Magician energy, which this time may not be used cleanly, but is in shadow for at least some of the time.

The Magician stands out for his capacity to observe and analyze, which means he can be adept at assessing weaknesses in himself and other people. This may lead to a lot of self- and other- criticism.

- Do you have a strong inner critic?
- Do you frequently criticize other people for their perceived failings?
- Are you overly critical of yourself and others?

Magician energy can be manipulative and turned to getting some personal advantage.

- Have you ever manipulated, dominated or gaslighted another person?
- Did you feel a sense of satisfaction or some other positive reward from that experience?
- Would you say that you can take pleasure from dominance over others, or from your ability to outwit them in intellectual debate?
- Is there a part of you that “preys on” other people, or indeed on yourself?
- Do you enjoy manipulating or dominating others?

A part of the Magician we can call the Safety Officer may form during childhood in response to the need to stay “one step ahead” of the people around you. Safety Officers are “always” on duty, because their role is to find ways of avoiding discomfort, danger, punishment or abuse. Once formed during childhood, they tend to stick around, even if they don’t really have an obvious role in adulthood. This may lead to someone feeling they are not achieving their full potential.

- Do you feel you have achieved less than you are capable of?
- Is there something (or some part of you) holding you back from doing so?
- If you believe there is, take a moment to consider how and why this part of you might have developed.
- Are you underachieving due to self-sabotage?
- What dangers did you face during childhood? Were you in danger of abuse, whether emotional, physical or sexual?
- How did you respond to this? How would you describe the part of you which grew up to protect you?
- Did childhood trauma shape your defense mechanisms?

An overactive Magician can take over the role of running your inner world. Unfortunately when this happens, the consequences can be uncomfortable. For example, since Magicians generally like to know they have the right answer, they may spend a long time analyzing different possibilities before making a decision, or they may repeatedly run over a problem in their minds, without coming to a conclusion.

- Do you lie awake at night mulling over problems?
- Do you obsessively overthink problems?

If you answered yes to many of these questions, it’s a sign your Magician energy is over-functioning.

### The Deflated Magician (The Denying Innocent One)

Sometimes a child may be shamed for being too clever, a show-off, or “bossy”. Sometimes a child learns very quickly that their natural intelligence is not welcome in the world they live in. This response to a child’s natural energy and enthusiasm can be very damaging to the expression of self, and may lead to the suppression of Magician energy.

That may appear to someone watching a child as “freezing”, confusion, “dumbness”, or some other way of hiding one’s natural abilities. These responses may be accompanied by a sense of what feels like embarrassment or, more exactly, shame.

- Do you feel exposed, embarrassed, or unable to cope under pressure?
- Do you buckle under pressure?
- Do you experience a lot of anxiety or nervousness when faced with situations where the attention may be mainly focused on you?
- Do you panic in the limelight?
- Have you ever found yourself unable to speak, shutting down mentally, and turning off your attention and daydreaming?
- Do you mentally shut down and dissociate?
- At school or later in life, have you ever been called a dreamer (or worse)?
- Are you labeled a daydreamer?
- Do you regard yourself as “thick”, under-educated, or intellectually inadequate?
- Do you feel intellectually inferior?

If you answered yes to these questions, it’s a sign your Magician energy is under-functioning.
