## MOVIE ASSIGNMENTS

Choose from one of the films listed below or find one of your own featuring a character who embodies the Magician archetype. Watch attentively, focusing on how the Magician archetype is portrayed. After viewing, complete the reflective exercises provided in the course to deepen your understanding and engage in shadow work.

### Recommended Magician Archetype Films (Sorted by IMDb Rating):

- **Arrival (2016)** — 7.9  
A linguist works to communicate with extraterrestrial visitors, demonstrating the Magician’s qualities of insight, wisdom, and bridging worlds.
- **Good Will Hunting (1997)** — 7.8  
Will Hunting is a wounded Magician, a troubled young genius whose struggles lead him to therapy with a functioning Magician in Sean Maguire (Robin Williams).
- **Star Wars (1977)** — 7.8  
Obi-Wan Kenobi epitomizes the Magician archetype, guiding and mentoring the young hero, Luke Skywalker, on his path to becoming a Jedi.
- **The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (2001)** — 7.8  
Gandalf the Grey represents the Magician archetype, possessing ancient wisdom, mystical powers, and serving as the guiding force for the Fellowship.
- **Doctor Sleep (2019)** — 7.4  
The sequel to The Shining, featuring a protagonist who uses psychic abilities and wisdom to confront darkness, illustrating the Magician’s healing and transformational role.
- **The Imitation Game (2014)** — 7.4  
Alan Turing, the wounded Magician, leads a team of cryptanalysts to crack the German Enigma code during World War II.
- **The Matrix (1999)** — 7.3  
Neo and Morpheus embody the Magician archetype as Neo discovers his extraordinary powers and leads a rebellion against oppressive machines.
- **The Shape of Water (2017)** — 7.3  
A mute woman forms a deep connection with a mysterious aquatic creature, reflecting the Magician’s transformative and healing powers.
- **Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them (2016)** — 7.3  
Newt Scamander embodies the Magician archetype through his knowledge of magical creatures and his role as a protector and guide.
- **Jurassic Park (1993)** — 7.1  
Dr. Alan Grant and Ian Malcolm embody the Magician archetype as knowledgeable and resourceful scientists navigating the challenges posed by resurrected dinosaurs.
- **Tree of Life (2011)** — 6.8  
Mr. O’Brien embodies the Magician archetype as a complex and authoritative figure deeply connected to the spiritual and existential aspects of life.
- **Dragonslayer (1981)** — 6.6  
A cartoonlike dramatization of initiation and the Magician archetype showing a young male hero discovering forces larger than himself, finding his “magus” mentor who introduces him to power equal to the challenge.
- **Doctor Strange (2016)** — 7.5  
A neurosurgeon discovers mystical powers and becomes the Sorcerer Supreme, embodying the Magician archetype through his quest for knowledge, transformation, and healing.
- **Embrace of the Serpent (2015)** — 6.7  
Karamakate, a shaman with mystical abilities, guides explorers in search of a rare healing plant, embodying the Magician archetype.
- **The Right Stuff (1983)** — 6.7  
Chuck Yeager embodies the Magician archetype as a fearless test pilot who breaks the sound barrier, symbolizing courage and determination.

### Reflective Exercise Prompts:

1. Describe one negative or “shadow” trait exhibited by the Magician character. How does this trait impact the story or other characters?
2. Reflect on a moment where you related to or were challenged by the Magician’s actions. What does this reveal about your own relationship with the archetype?
3. Identify a person who embodies similar Magician qualities. How do you respond to their energy or advice?
4. Identify one positive quality you’d like to develop or one shadow aspect to address. How might you begin this integration?
