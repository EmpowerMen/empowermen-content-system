### Create Your Warrior Energy Action Plan

Welcome to Chapter 5, the final part of the Warrior Within Module.

In previous chapters, we have explored your shadow — the dark, repressed side of the Warrior archetype within your psyche. The way out of feelings of cowardice, aggression, or passivity lies in facing your shadow — bringing light and awareness to your unconscious behaviors, patterns, and limiting beliefs. Now it’s time to create an action plan to retrieve and awaken the Warrior energy within that has been lost in your shadow.

### Embodying the Warrior in His Fullness

The Warrior in his fullness embodies several key qualities. Consider these as potential aims for your own journey:

- **Do something that scares you:** Challenge yourself regularly, from speaking up in meetings to daring adventures like skydiving.
- **Be more decisive:** Practice making quick, firm decisions in daily life, even in small matters.
- **Quit seeking exits:** Commit fully to your chosen path, resisting the urge to look for easy ways out.
- **Find your core values:** Identify and articulate the principles that guide your life and actions.
- **Have a plan and purpose:** Set clear goals and create a roadmap for achieving them.
- **Strengthen your resilience:** Develop mental toughness through regular challenges and self-reflection.
- **Find out who you are loyal to:** Clarify your allegiances and ensure they align with your values.
- **Establish and live via non-negotiable terms:** Set clear boundaries in your life and relationships.
- **Compete:** Engage regularly in healthy competition to push yourself and others to excel.
- **Strengthen your discipline via habits and routine:** Develop a structured daily routine that supports your goals.
- **Adopt a minimalist attitude:** Focus on what’s truly essential, decluttering physically and mentally.
- **Take up a physical discipline:** Consider martial arts or other practices combining mental and physical discipline.

Reflect on which of these aims resonate most with you. Trust your instincts and make a note of these in your workbook.

### Creating a Basic Action Plan

Follow this step-by-step process and turn to Workbook Exercise 4 in your Warrior Within workbook:

- **Step One: Ignite Your Insights**  
Reflect on your previous workbook notes, recognizing your progress and preparing mentally for the next steps.
- **Step Two: Target Your Warrior Energy**  
Identify areas to improve based on shadow work, energy wheel gaps, and your assessment notes. Choose a personalized aim (e.g., “Adopt a minimalist attitude,” “Develop my boundary setting,” “Handle my rage better”). Write a short, memorable phrase.
- **Step Three: Activate Your Arsenal**  
Choose actions or practices to support your aim. Use the provided list and community suggestions. Note your chosen practices in your workbook.
- **Step Four: Embrace the Challenge**  
Face your fears and push beyond comfort zones. Explore areas you might initially repel from — these are likely to yield the most growth.
- **Step Five: Strategize Your Warrior Week**  
Plan a week to explore your Warrior energy. Schedule times for actions, watch a Warrior archetype movie, use Active Imagination and Dream Work resources, and engage with the EmpowerMen Community.
- **Step Six: Adapt Your Warrior Way**  
Be flexible and open-minded. Adjust your plan as you discover what works best. Embrace experimentation and trust your instincts.
