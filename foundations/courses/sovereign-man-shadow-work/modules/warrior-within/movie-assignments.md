## MOVIE ASSIGNMENTS

Choose from one of the films listed below or find one of your own featuring a character who embodies the Warrior archetype. Watch the film attentively, focusing on how the Warrior archetype is portrayed. After viewing, complete the reflective exercises provided in the course to deepen your understanding and engage in shadow work.

### Recommended Warrior Archetype Films (Sorted by IMDb Rating):

- **Seven Samurai (1954)** — 8.6  
Japanese exploration of the warrior archetype and teamwork, the original version of The Magnificent Seven.
- **Apocalypse Now (1979)** — 8.4  
Depicts erotic contamination in Warrior energy through Robert Duvall’s air cavalry commander, showing the connection between warrior energy, eroticization, and aliveness at the edge of danger.
- **American History X (1998)** — 8.5  
Edward Norton plays Derek Vinyard, a former neo-Nazi skinhead seeking redemption.
- **Platoon (1986)** — 8.1  
Shows the Warrior archetype’s shadow side with GIs opening fire on a helpless Vietnamese village, depicting battle frenzy and loss of control.
- **Logan (2017)** — 8.1  
Hugh Jackman stars as an aging mutant warrior protecting a young girl.
- **Patton (1970)** — 7.9  
Features General Patton as a warrior archetype in fullness, including iconic combat scenes and his connection to his calling.
- **Fight Club (1999)** — 7.8  
Brad Pitt and Edward Norton star in a film about rebellion and challenging societal norms, with Tyler Durden embodying the Warrior archetype.
- **The Last Samurai (2003)** — 7.7  
Tom Cruise portrays Nathan Algren, who embraces Samurai culture and fights for honor.
- **The Magnificent Seven (1960)** — 7.7  
Demonstrates knighthood and the knight/warrior archetype, emphasizing alliances between warriors.
- **Braveheart (1995)** — 7.6  
Mel Gibson stars as William Wallace, leading a rebellion against English tyranny.
- **Falling Down (1993)** — 7.6  
Michael Douglas plays William Foster, an ordinary man confronting societal flaws.
- **Gladiator (2000)** — 7.5  
Russell Crowe portrays Maximus, a former Roman General seeking justice and retribution.
- **Kingdom of Heaven (2005)** — 7.2  
Orlando Bloom depicts Balian of Ibelin defending Jerusalem during the Crusades.
- **The Great Santini (1979)** — 7.2  
Robert Duvall portrays a Marine fighter pilot demonstrating the destructiveness of Warrior energy in family relationships.
- **MacArthur (1977)** — 7.1  
Gregory Peck stars as General Douglas MacArthur, showing strategic brilliance.
- **Top Gun (1986)** — 6.9  
A film about transforming a hero into a warrior.

### Reflective Exercise Prompts:

1. Describe one negative or “shadow” trait exhibited by the Warrior character. How does this trait impact the story or other characters?
2. Reflect on a moment where you related to or were challenged by the Warrior’s actions. What does this reveal about your own relationship with this archetype?
3. Identify a person who embodies similar Warrior qualities. How do you respond to their energy or advice?
4. Identify one positive quality you’d like to develop or one shadow aspect to address. How might you begin this integration?
