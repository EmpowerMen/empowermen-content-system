# Welcome to the Sovereign Man Course

*A practical 60-day journey to reclaim your sovereignty*

---

## Course Overview

The Sovereign Man course is a focused, step-by-step program designed to help you master the four core masculine archetypes — King, Warrior, Magician, and Lover. Through video lessons, self-assessments, shadow work, and practical exercises, you will build lasting habits and create a personalized Sovereignty Plan that guides your daily life.

---

## How the Course Works

Each archetype module consists of:

- **Learning & Inner Work:** Video lessons, self-assessment, shadow exploration, and exercises.
- **Action Plan Trial Week:** A dedicated week to try out the aims and practices you’ve chosen based on your inner work insights. This is your chance to experiment and discover what truly supports your growth.

---

## Why 60 Days?

- **Depth and Transformation:** This course is not a quick fix. It’s a comprehensive journey that requires time to absorb powerful concepts, engage in meaningful inner work, and integrate new practices into your life.
- **Balanced Pace:** 60 days (about 8 weeks) provides a realistic timeframe to balance learning and application without overwhelm. It allows you to build momentum and sustain progress.
- **Clear Milestones:** The course is structured with clear milestones — mastering one archetype every two weeks — to keep you focused and motivated.

## How to Approach the Course

- **Daily Learning:** Each archetype module contains 5 chapters. Aim to complete one chapter per day during the learning phase to maintain steady progress.
- **Action Plan Trial Weeks:** After completing the chapters, dedicate a full week to try out the aims and practices you’ve chosen. This experimentation phase helps you discover what works best for you.
- **Reflection & Adjustment:** Use daily “Pause and Reflect” exercises to build awareness and adjust your practices as needed.
- **Community Engagement:** Participate in forums, live Q&A sessions, and accountability groups to stay motivated and supported.

---

## After completing all four archetypes, you will:

- **Integrate, Commit & Grow:** Choose one primary aim and one or two supporting actions from each archetype to create a balanced, manageable Sovereignty Plan.
- **Engage with Community:** Share your plan, seek support, and establish sustainable leadership habits.

---

## What You Will Gain

- **Clarity:** Deep understanding of your archetypal strengths and shadows.
- **Practical Tools:** Daily practices that build discipline, emotional intelligence, and connection.
- **Personalized Sovereignty Plan:** A focused, manageable plan tailored to your life and goals.
- **Support Network:** Access to a brotherhood of men committed to growth and accountability.

## Tips for Success

- **Commit to the Full 60 Days:** Transformation takes time—consistency is key.
- **Engage Fully:** Complete daily chapters and action plan exercises with intention.
- **Be Honest:** Shadow work requires courage and openness to growth.
- **Use Your Community:** Share your journey and learn from others’ experiences.
- **Adapt Your Plan:** Your Sovereignty Plan is a living document—refine it as you evolve.
