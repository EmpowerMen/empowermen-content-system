# King Exemplars

Curated quotes from real, named men whose lives demonstrate traits of the
King archetype in its fullness — just ordering, blessing, responsibility,
and generative leadership. Companion library to
`/foundations/robert-moore-lectures/` and `/foundations/sources/misc/warrior-exemplars.md`.

King David and, more loosely, Winston Churchill and Abraham Lincoln are
referenced by name in Dr. Robert Moore's lecture transcripts as real-world
illustrations of King psychology — Moore names Churchill and Lincoln as
"positive expressions" of the archetype in `the-king-within.md`, and
discusses King David at length across `king-david.md` as the archetype
integrating all four masculine energies. Marcus Aurelius and Nelson
Mandela are not referenced in the Moore transcripts currently in this
repo; they're included here as standalone exemplars of the same traits.
