# Warrior Exemplars

Curated quotes from real, named men whose lives demonstrate traits of the
Warrior archetype in its fullness — courage, discipline, boundary-setting,
decisive action, and service to a cause beyond self. Intended as a
companion library to `/foundations/robert-moore-lectures/` for content
that wants to ground the archetype in recognizable historical figures
rather than theory alone.

Two of the men below (Malcolm X, Martin Luther King Jr., Winston
Churchill, Douglas MacArthur) are referenced by name by Dr. Robert Moore
in his lecture transcripts as real-world illustrations of Warrior
psychology — note that Moore's discussion of MacArthur and Churchill in
particular leans as much into Warrior/King shadow (narcissism, inflation)
as it does pure exemplar status, so pair any citation of Moore alongside
these quotes with that context. Muhammad Ali is not referenced in the
Moore transcripts currently in this repo; he's included here as a
standalone exemplar.
