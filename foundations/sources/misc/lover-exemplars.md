# Lover Exemplars

Curated quotes from real, named men whose lives demonstrate traits of the
Lover archetype in its fullness — passion, connection, sensual aliveness,
and appreciation of beauty. Companion library to
`/foundations/robert-moore-lectures/` and `/foundations/sources/misc/warrior-exemplars.md`.

All six men below are named directly by Dr. Robert Moore in the
`the-lover-within.md` transcript, where he lists deceased "high priests"
of Lover energy: "Richard Burton, Jimi Hendrix, Jim Morrison, Elvis
Presley... Louis Armstrong... Chuck Berry... John Lennon. Vincent Van
Gogh. Casanova... Oscar Wilde." Note that Moore frames these as men who
embody the raw intensity of the archetype, not necessarily balanced
examples of it — several (Hendrix, Morrison, Van Gogh) he elsewhere ties
to the archetype's dangers ("it's no accident that so many rock stars
burn up and die"). Pair any citation of Moore alongside these quotes with
that context.
