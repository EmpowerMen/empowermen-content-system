# David the King - A Psychological Perspective

## Lecture Context

This workshop by Dr. Robert Moore explores the mythic and psychological dimensions of the biblical figure David, focusing on his role as a paradigmatic man embodying the complexities of the masculine archetypes. The lecture examines the tensions within David’s character, the struggle for establishing a sacred center, and the interplay of power, love, and shadow in the masculine psyche.
